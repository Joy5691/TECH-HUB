Our Story

TECH HUB was created as part of our CSE347 course project with the vision of building a modern and user-friendly technology platform. The goal of this project is to demonstrate practical skills in web development while addressing real-world needs in the tech retail space.

Through TECH HUB, we aim to showcase a wide range of technology products, including laptops, smartphones, gaming accessories, and other essential tech items. This project reflects our focus on clean design, usability, and efficient system structure.

TECH HUB represents our learning journey, teamwork, and passion for technology. By developing this website, we strive to combine academic knowledge with practical implementation, creating a platform that is both functional and visually engaging.
